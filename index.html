About關於我

Hi, I'm Luke.嗨，我是林吉祥。

I'm a teacher and the founder of My Culture Connect, a nonprofit that brings free English education to rural schools in Taiwan. My days move between three things I love — running the organization, teaching, and creating learning materials.

Lately I've fallen for something new: using AI as a teaching partner. It changed how fast I can build things for my students — and I think every teacher deserves to feel that. So I made this site to pass it on.

我是一位老師，也是 人師教育協會（My Culture Connect）的創辦人——一個在台灣偏鄉提供免費英語教育的非營利組織。我的日常在三件我熱愛的事之間：經營協會、教學、製作教材。

最近我迷上了一件新事：把 AI 當作教學的夥伴。它讓我為學生做教材的速度完全不一樣了——我覺得每位老師都值得體會這種感覺。所以做了這個網站，想把它傳下去。

Keep going繼續走下去

This is just the beginning這只是開始

If you stay with it, this gets exciting. The same simple skills — a webpage, a little AI — grow into things you might not believe you can do yet: a sign-up form on your own site, a way to collect and check your students' quiz answers, educational games they'll actually want to play. I'm learning these too — and every step I take, I'll share right here.

只要你願意繼續,接下來會越來越精彩。同樣是那些簡單的技能——一個網頁、一點 AI——會慢慢長成你現在可能還不敢相信自己做得到的事:在自己的網站上做報名表、收集並批改學生的測驗作答、設計出學生真的想玩的教育遊戲。這些我也還在學——而我每學會一步,就會在這裡分享出來。
