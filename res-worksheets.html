Worksheets for Every Level分級學習單製作

Here's the honest secret: making a worksheet with AI is mostly just asking well. Once you know the few things to include in your request, you can produce a worksheet for any topic, at any level, with an answer key — in under a minute.

老實說個祕密:用 AI 做學習單,其實大半就是把指令下好。只要知道要求裡該包含哪幾項,你就能在一分鐘內,為任何主題、任何程度產出一份附答案的學習單。

The recipe配方

Six things to put in your request指令裡要放的六樣東西

Include these and the AI rarely gets it wrong:把這幾項放進去,AI 很少會做錯:

  • Topic — "present simple", "animal vocabulary", "telling time"主題——「現在簡單式」「動物單字」「看時鐘」
  • Level — grade, age, or CEFR (A1 / A2 / B1)程度——年級、年齡,或 CEFR(A1 / A2 / B1)
  • Skill / format — fill-in-the-blank, matching, short answer技能 / 題型——填空、配對、簡答
  • Length — "8 questions", "one A4 page"長度——「8 題」「一張 A4」
  • Answer key — "include the answers at the end"答案——「最後附上答案」
  • Extras — a title, a name/date line, simple instructions附加——標題、姓名/日期欄、簡單作答說明
Set the level設定程度

How to control difficulty怎麼控制難度

The level is the part most teachers under-specify. Pick whichever fits how you think:程度是多數老師講得最不夠清楚的地方。挑一個你習慣的方式講就好:

  • By grade or age — "for grade 2", "for 8-year-olds"用年級或年齡——「給二年級」「給 8 歲」
  • By CEFR — "A1 beginner", "A2", "B1"用 CEFR——「A1 初學」「A2」「B1」
  • By constraint — "only words from this list", "sentences under 6 words"用限制條件——「只用這份清單的字」「句子不超過 6 個字」
  • By comparison — "make it easier than this", "make it harder"用比較——「比這個再簡單一點」「再難一點」
Copy this直接複製

One topic, three levels一個主題,三種程度

Paste into Gemini (or any AI). Change the words in bold:貼進 Gemini(或任何 AI),改掉粗體的字:

Make three worksheets on the present simple
tense, same topic, three levels:

1. A1 beginner — 8 fill-in-the-blank questions
2. A2 — 8 questions, mix of blanks and short answers
3. B1 — 8 questions, write full sentences

For each: add a title, a Name and Date line,
one line of simple instructions, and an answer
key at the end. Keep each to one A4 page.
Tip:小訣竅: ask for all three levels in one request — now you have a ready-made set for a mixed-ability class. 一次要求三種程度——你立刻就有一套適合混合程度班級的現成學習單。
Get it out輸出

Printable, or a webpage可列印,或做成網頁

  • To print: copy the text into Google Docs or Word, tidy it, and print要列印:把文字複製到 Google Docs 或 Word,整理一下再印
  • As a webpage: ask for it as an HTML file and publish it (Lesson 1)做成網頁:要求它輸出成 HTML 檔,再發佈(第一課)
  • As a quiz: ask for an interactive HTML version your students can click做成測驗:要求一個學生能點的互動 HTML 版本

Quick recap快速回顧

  • A worksheet is mostly a well-written request學習單大半就是一句寫得好的指令
  • Include topic, level, format, length, answer key, extras放入:主題、程度、題型、長度、答案、附加項
  • Set the level by grade, CEFR, constraint, or comparison用年級、CEFR、限制或比較來設定程度
  • Ask for several levels at once for mixed classes為混合程度班級一次要求多種程度
  • Print it, or publish it as a page列印,或發佈成網頁
