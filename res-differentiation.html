Differentiated Teaching差異化教學

Differentiation usually means hours of extra work — making the same lesson three times for three groups. This is where AI quietly shines: one topic in, several tailored versions out. Let AI produce the lesson plans and worksheets; you keep the judgement.

差異化通常代表多花好幾個小時——同一課為三個小組各做一次。這正是 AI 默默發光的地方:丟進一個主題,產出好幾個量身版本。讓 AI 產出教案與學習單,判斷仍然在你手上。

The idea核心觀念

Same goal, different routes同樣的目標,不同的路

Differentiation keeps the learning goal the same while changing the route to it for different students. AI can take one lesson and spin out those routes in seconds — support, core, and stretch versions, or versions for different kinds of learners.差異化是讓學習目標不變,但為不同學生改變抵達目標的路徑。AI 能把一課在幾秒內變出這些路徑——支持版、核心版、挑戰版,或為不同類型學生量身的版本。

Three angles三個切入角度

Three ways to differentiate三種差異化方式

By readiness依程度

Support, core, and stretch versions — same task, three difficulty levels.

支持版、核心版、挑戰版——同一個任務,三種難度。

By learning profile依學習風格

Versions for visual, hands-on, or read-write learners.

為視覺型、動手型、讀寫型學生分別做版本。

By interest依興趣

Same skill, themed around sports, animals, or music.

同樣的技能,換成運動、動物或音樂主題。

Prompts that differentiate for you幫你做差異化的提示詞

Tiered worksheets分層學習單

Take this worksheet and give me three versions
of it: a SUPPORT version (simpler, more scaffolding),
a CORE version, and a STRETCH version (more challenge).
Keep the same topic and learning goal. Add an answer
key to each.

A differentiated lesson plan差異化教案

Write a 40-minute lesson plan on telling
the time for a mixed-ability grade 3 class.
Include: one shared warm-up, then tiered tasks for
support / core / stretch groups, a hands-on option
and a visual option, plus a simple way to check
everyone met the goal.

Keep the judgement判斷在你

AI drafts, you decideAI 出草稿,你來決定

AI is fast at producing versions, but you know your students. Skim each version, adjust the level if a group needs it, and swap in topics your class actually cares about. The time you save on typing goes back into teaching.AI 產版本很快,但了解學生的是你。每個版本掃過一遍,某組需要就調整難度,把主題換成你的班級真正在意的東西。省下打字的時間,回到教學本身。

Quick recap快速回顧

  • Differentiation = same goal, different routes差異化 = 同樣目標,不同路徑
  • Differentiate by readiness, learning profile, or interest依程度、學習風格或興趣做差異化
  • Ask for support / core / stretch versions in one go一次要求支持 / 核心 / 挑戰三種版本
  • Get whole tiered lesson plans, not just worksheets不只學習單,連整份分層教案都能要
