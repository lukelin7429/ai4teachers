Picture Books with Gemini用 Gemini 做繪本

For an illustrated story, you don't need design skills — Google's Gemini has a Picture Book Gem made for exactly this. You give it a story idea; it writes the words and draws the pictures, page by page.

想做一本有插圖的故事書,你不需要設計能力——Google 的 Gemini 有一個專門做這件事的 Picture Book Gem。你給它一個故事點子,它就一頁一頁地寫文字、畫插圖。

First, what's a Gem?先說,Gem 是什麼?

A Gem is a ready-made helperGem 是一個現成的小幫手

Gemini's "Gems" are versions of the AI set up for one job. The Picture Book Gem is set up to create illustrated stories — so you skip the long instructions and just tell it your idea.Gemini 的「Gems」是為了某一件工作而設定好的 AI 版本。Picture Book Gem 就是專門用來做有插圖的故事——所以你不用打一長串指令,直接講你的點子就好。

Don't see a Picture Book Gem in your list? You can make your own in a minute: in Gemini, open Gems → New Gem, name it "Picture Book", and tell it "You create illustrated children's picture books, one page at a time, with simple text and a matching picture for each page." 清單裡沒看到 Picture Book Gem?你可以一分鐘自己做一個:在 Gemini 裡開 Gems → New Gem,命名「Picture Book」,然後告訴它「你會製作兒童繪本,一次一頁,每頁有簡單的文字和對應的插圖。」
Step by step一步一步

Making your book做出你的繪本

  1. Open the Picture Book Gem打開 Picture Book Gem In Gemini, go to Gems and choose the Picture Book one (or your own).在 Gemini 裡進入 Gems,選 Picture Book 那個(或你自己做的)。
  2. Tell it your story告訴它你的故事 Give it the idea, the age group, the language level, the main character, and the lesson or value. e.g. "A shy turtle who learns to ask for help, for 6-year-olds, very simple English."給它點子、年齡層、語言程度、主角、以及課程或想傳達的價值。例如:「一隻害羞的烏龜學會開口求助,給 6 歲小朋友,非常簡單的英文。」
  3. Let it draft page by page讓它一頁一頁產出 It returns each page with text and a picture. Read along and react.它會給出每一頁的文字和插圖。一邊看一邊回應。
  4. Revise anything you don't like不滿意的地方就改 Just say "make page 3 simpler" or "draw the turtle smiling". Keep going until it's right.直接說「第 3 頁簡單一點」或「把烏龜畫成微笑」。改到滿意為止。
Make it teach讓它更能教

Tips for the classroom課堂上的小訣竅

  • State the level clearly — "one short sentence per page, A1 English"把程度講清楚——「每頁一個短句,A1 英文」
  • Ask for a target vocabulary set to be used naturally指定一組目標單字,讓它自然地用進去
  • Ask for bilingual captions if you want Chinese under the English想要英文下面有中文,就要求雙語對照
  • Ask it to keep the character consistent across pages要求主角形象前後一致
  • End with 3 simple comprehension questions最後加 3 個簡單的理解問題
Use it怎麼用

What to do with the finished book做好之後可以怎麼用

  • Project it as a read-along during class課堂上投影出來,帶著一起讀
  • Print it as a little book or worksheet印出來當小書或學習單
  • Save the pictures and put them on a webpage (Lesson 1)把圖片存下來,放到網頁上(第一課)
A gentle note: AI pictures aren't perfect — characters can drift between pages, and fine details (like text inside a picture) can look odd. Skim every page before you use it with children. 溫馨提醒:AI 的圖不完美——主角在不同頁可能長得不太一樣,細節(像圖裡的文字)也可能怪怪的。給孩子看之前,每頁都先掃一遍。

Quick recap快速回顧

  • A Gem is a ready-made Gemini helper — use the Picture Book oneGem 是現成的 Gemini 幫手——用 Picture Book 那個
  • Give it the idea, age, level, character, and lesson給它點子、年齡、程度、主角、課程目標
  • Revise page by page until it fits your class一頁一頁修到適合你的班級
  • Project, print, or put it on a webpage投影、列印,或放到網頁上
  • Always check the pages before class上課前一定先檢查每一頁
