Free Resources & Festivals免費資源與節慶教學

Here's a relief: you don't have to make everything from scratch. A lot of ready-made bilingual teaching material already exists for free — especially for festivals and holidays. Start there, then use AI to adapt it to your class.

這是個好消息:你不需要什麼都從零開始做。很多現成的雙語教材早就免費存在了——尤其是節慶和假日主題。先從那裡開始,再用 AI 把它調整成適合你班級的版本。

The Changhua Bilingual resource network彰化雙語資源網

There's a free, ready-made bilingual resource site built for exactly this — lessons, festival pages, word-of-the-day, and more, made for Taiwanese classrooms. For festival and holiday themes especially, look here before building anything yourself.有一個免費、現成的雙語資源網站,就是為這件事做的——課程、節慶頁面、每日一字等等,專為台灣的課堂設計。尤其是節慶和假日主題,動手自己做之前,先來這裡看看。

Why festivals為什麼是節慶

Festivals are the easy win節慶是最好上手的

Holiday and festival themes come back every year, the vocabulary is concrete and fun, and students already have something to say. That makes them the perfect place to use ready-made resources instead of starting from a blank page.節慶和假日主題每年都會回來,單字具體又有趣,學生本來就有話可說。所以這是最適合直接用現成資源、而不必從空白頁開始的地方。

  • Reusable every year — build once, use forever每年都能重複用——做一次,用很久
  • Concrete, picture-friendly vocabulary具體、好配圖的單字
  • Students are already motivated to join in學生本來就有動機參與
Make it yours改成你的

Adapt what you find with AI用 AI 調整你找到的資源

Found a resource that's close but not quite right? Paste it into AI and adjust it:找到一個很接近、但還不夠合適的資源?把它貼進 AI 調整一下:

Here is a festival lesson. Please:
- simplify it for grade 2,
- shorten it to 20 minutes,
- and add 5 vocabulary words with simple pictures.

Then turn it into a game (the Games page), a worksheet (the Worksheets page), or a webpage to share (Lesson 1).接著把它變成遊戲(教育遊戲那頁)、學習單(學習單那頁),或可分享的網頁(第一課)。
